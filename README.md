# Unsent: The Micro-Confession Sprint

An entry for make.fun's [Unsent: The Micro-Confession Sprint](https://make.fun/bounty/u-boothlord-unsent-the-micro-confession-sprint-8c6596ba) bounty ($2, top 2 each win $1).

## The entry

A two-part micro-confession, exactly as it was typed and never sent:

```
To: Dad
I sold the guitar you gave me when I was sixteen, and every time I hear one now I wish I had kept the one thing you were proud of me for.
```

- Full text: 145 characters, 2 lines.
- To: line: `To: Dad` (7 characters).
- Message line: 137 characters, one line, no explanation after it.

Why it stayed in drafts: the regret is specific and heavy — selling the one thing his father was proud of him for — and the words are honest rather than general. The To: line and the message need each other: "the one thing you were proud of me for" only lands when the addressee is Dad.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the confession to `/output/entry.txt` and asserts the gate: full text 1–260 characters, exactly two lines, first line a `To:` line, message line at most 200 characters, not only whitespace.
